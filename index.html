About me

I’m Bharath, an engineer working in cloud infrastructure and low-latency Linux systems. I spend most of my time deep in AWS and the Linux kernel, working on CPU power management, NUMA, kernel bypass, and the network data path. These are the parts of the stack most people treat as a black box.

What I enjoy most is turning “it’s slow sometimes” into a measured, reproducible answer. I like building things end to end: forming a hypothesis, instrumenting the system, gathering the evidence, and only then drawing a conclusion, then writing it up so someone else can reproduce it.

Outside of the day job I tinker with bare-metal performance, DPDK, and whatever rabbit hole looks interesting that week. When I learn something worth sharing, it ends up here.
